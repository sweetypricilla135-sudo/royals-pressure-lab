Methodology & interpretation

This dashboard displays the results of the standalone SupeRR Selector analysis. High pressure is defined from a pre-delivery match-state heuristic; batting and bowling impacts are measured relative to contextual expected performance. The model shrinks high-pressure impact using sample size. Eligibility requires sufficient overall, ordinary and high-pressure exposure.

Scale: adjusted impact = high-pressure impact × high-pressure balls ÷ (high-pressure balls + 100). The six-ball display multiplies this by 6; it is not actual scoring rate or economy. Batting impact is batter runs above contextual expectation. Bowling impact combines runs prevented and wickets valued at 8 runs above expectation, on legal balls only; wides and no-balls are excluded. Batting observations exclude wides but include no-balls, so six observations are only approximately an over.
Evidence: eligibility requires ≥500 balls, ≥20 matches, ≥100 high and ordinary balls, and ≥10 matches in each category. Qualified players also have positive high impact and improvement. Low-sample estimates remain visible but are not qualified recommendations.
Interpretation: Pressure score is a model-derived indicator, not a prediction of IPL 2027 performance. Phase-adjusted improvement is a diagnostic comparison within comparable innings phases; where missing, the evidence is insufficient for that check. Positive-match percentage is descriptive, not a formal statistical confidence interval. Selected players are not necessarily available at the IPL 2027 auction. The IPL-screening decision is separate from statistical performance.
